# 0010. Decisions of the product owner, 19/11/2026

- Status: accepted, 2026-11-19
- Context: `1.0.1-beta.2` shipped on the beta channel on 19/11/2026, and the build of 1.1.0 (typo correction,
  `spec/01-engine.md` section 11A, PRD-R4A) started on `feat/v110-api` with the open questions built as recommended.
  The integrator then asked the product owner four questions: Q10 and Q11 of `spec/01-engine.md` section 15, the
  mid-line reset blind spot of the 1.1.0 plan, and the provisional call on CLR-R13 that both betas of 1.0.1 shipped
  under. The product owner accepted every recommendation. The answers are below in neutral English, as the wording
  decision of 15/11/2026 in [0005](0005-pa-decisions-2026-11-13.md) asks.
- Requirements touched: ENG-R1111, ENG-R1116, ENG-R850, PRD-R80, CLR-R13.

## The product owner's answers

| # | Topic | Decision | Implemented in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Q10, a word in sentence case at the start of a sentence (ENG-R1111) | Corrected, as any other word: `Androdi` at a sentence start gives `Android`. A name that starts a sentence and is one edit from a common word is corrected once, until the restore key adds it to the never-correct list; the guard list of proper names covers most names. The D30 copy keeps `từ viết hoa toàn bộ` / `words in all capitals`. | `spec/01-engine.md` section 15; `spec/03-product.md` PRD-R80; `CorrectionPolicy.sentenceStart = .corrected` (1.1.0, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-typo-correction.md` Task 1) |
| 2 | Q11, correction at Return and Tab (ENG-R1116) | Decided at every key that commits, Return and Tab included, as restore and D29 are (P0-3, 13.9.1). A chat message sent right after a typo leaves corrected, and the restore key cannot undo it once Return has cleared the history; Backspace and retyping still work in the next message. | `spec/01-engine.md` section 15; `CorrectionPolicy.commitKeys = .everyCommitKey` (plan Task 1); the Tab case in plan Task 14 |
| 3 | The mid-line reset blind spot | 1.1.0 ships with it. After a click or a focus change the engine's history is empty, so a word typed right after a `#`, `@` or `/` the engine never saw is a candidate (`#androdi` typed after clicking into the middle of a line gives `#android`), the same blind spot rule L9 has (ENG-R850). It is pinned by `TypoCorrectionContextTests.afterAResetTheWordIsACandidate` and listed under Known issues in the changelogs. Telling the engine the character before the caret after a reset (one Accessibility read per reset, PLT-R304 permitting) stays a 1.x candidate. | Plan Review Focus 2 and Task 33; `CHANGELOG.md` and `CHANGELOG.vi.md` of 1.1.0 |
| 4 | A pending CLR-R13 comparison | Confirmed: a comparison that is commissioned but not finished does not block a release by itself; the release's provenance report records that it is pending, and a `fail` verdict blocks. This was the integrator's call of 18/11/2026 ([0009](0009-pa-decisions-2026-11-18.md), "Integrator's calls"); `1.0.1-beta.1` and `1.0.1-beta.2` shipped under it, and the stable 1.0.1 may ship under it. | `spec/05-clean-room.md` CLR-R13 amendment; [0009](0009-pa-decisions-2026-11-18.md) amendment; [provenance report of 1.0.1](../provenance/reports/2026-11-18-1.0.1.md), note of 2026-11-19 |

## Consequences

- The 1.1.0 plan builds what was decided: no code changes, because Stage 0 already built Q10 and Q11 as
  recommended. The plan's list of open decisions keeps the copy review (when the user interface track lands) and
  the release sequencing.
- Stable 1.0.1 does not wait for the appointed reviewer of the similarity review. Note N1 of the 1.0.1 provenance
  report stays open until a similarity report exists, and its delta must reach the commit tagged 1.0.1.
