# Reference model (test oracle, not shipped)

A small Python model of the orthography and composition rules in `../01-engine.md`, written by the author of that file from the spec text only (no other input method's code was consulted). It generated the corpus counts quoted in 01-engine.md section 3 and checked the worked examples.

- `ortho.py`: syllable inventory, tiers, tone rules.
- `compose.py`: keystroke-to-display composition (Telex, VNI, both tone styles).
- `boundary.py`: commit / restore decisions; `common_en.txt` is a hand-written English fixture list. `type_run` models the English run of ENG-R823 as amended on 14/11/2026 (an `en` token and no `vi` token among the previous three; a token kept by L11 is neutral), and `python3 -I boundary.py` checks rows B065-B072 of 13.9.2 and the sentences of ENG-R823 (none expected to differ). `type_run_collision` adds the collision rules at commit of 15/11/2026 (L9a boundary particle, L9b phrase looking back, the `run: vi` exemption of L10 and the English-lean window; ENG-R823, ENG-R825 to ENG-R827) with small fixture lists, and checks `COLLISION_RUNS`; the revisions of ENG-R828 are not modelled. `type_run` with `context="code"` models rule L9 as amended on 15/11/2026 (ENG-R850, REG-35: a code-only word is restored only outside Vietnamese text, read from the line before the token) with the product owner's sentence condition of 16/11/2026 (the first word of its line, after nothing but spaces and the openers of prose, in sentence case, committed by a space and not on the capitalized code-word list, is decided by the rules after L9 at its own commit, with the English run of the line before ended), and RV5 of ENG-R828 as added on 16/11/2026 (REG-36: a code-only word that L9 restored as the first word of its line takes its Vietnamese display when the next word is Vietnamese, the only revision the model covers), and checks `CODE_RUNS`, which hold rows B035 to B035e, B035i to B035n and B064a to B064c of 13.9 and 13.9.1, the sentences of ENG-R850, the sentence condition and the RV5 guards, `CODE_RUNS_WITHOUT_LINE_REVISION` (`lineStartRevision` off) and `CODE_RUNS_CAPITALIZED` (`tar` on the capitalized code-word list, standing for the bundled `host`). The 13.9 fixture lists are split as in the spec: `TECH` is the tech-always list, `CODE_ONLY` the code-only list, which holds `chown`, a word whose keys are not English, since the product owner decided on 18/11/2026 that for such a word L9 is evaluated right after L5, before L6 and L7 (ENG-R801, ENG-R850, REG-38; `CODE_RUNS` rows B035o to B035t). `decide` models rule L4 as amended by the product owner on 17/11/2026 (ENG-R804, REG-37: doublings, the keys of the escaped form typed alone, vowel groups and an escape as the last key; `compose.py` records `escape_across` and `last_escape`) and the escape condition of L3 (ENG-R801: `www` commits `www`), and checks `ESCAPE_RUNS`; the phrase evidence of ENG-R804 (a) is not modelled, and with the 13.9 lists no row has both forms English. `decide` also models rule L4a as decided by the product owner on 18/11/2026 (ENG-R411, REG-39: a token whose last key removed the tone or the marks keeps the text shown unless its keys are English; `compose.py` records `last_removal`) and checks `REMOVAL_RUNS` and `REMOVAL_VNI`, rows B052a to B052d included.
- `corpus.py`: generates the O1-O4 corpus; `examples.py` checks its example list (and writes `examples.json`) and every 13.8 row of `rows.json` (`python3 -I examples.py` prints the mismatches; none expected).
- `compose.py` models Backspace by ENG-R710 as amended on 14/11/2026 (a modifier key shared with a letter that stays keeps marking it, REG-31) and the space boundary with re-open (ENG-R1002) for rows W243h and W243i; the restore decisions of section 8 are left to `boundary.py`. It also models the amendments of 15/11/2026: tone removal by layers (ENG-R411: the tone, then every mark and `đ`, REG-34), Backspace on an escape letter with the keys it undid and its fallbacks (ENG-R711, rows W241, W242, W242a-W242w: tone keys that wait for their vowel in the replay, a standalone `w` letter shown plain kept as a plain key, the letters shown retyped), the shared-key rule counting only mark keys after a mark removal (ENG-R710, W242s, W242t), and a removal key that would replay as a letter being left out (ENG-R710). The bracket doubling escape (4.8) is not modelled. Rows W243k-W243o (a re-opened restored word, ENG-R1005) are not in `rows.json`: they depend on the restore decisions of section 8.

Known gaps (update the model from the spec before relying on it):

1. It predates two review additions: rhyme `êc` after `gi` (`giếc`, ENG-R305) and open `y` after `th` (`Thy`, ENG-R314).
2. It predates the product owner's P0 additions: the `dz` onset (01-engine.md 3.3, ENG-R316), worked examples W249-W253 and boundary rows B053-B064.
3. It counts the spelling `giu` twice: once as onset `g` + rhyme `iu` and once as onset `gi` + rhyme `u`. The normative parse of 01-engine.md 3.13 reads `giu` only as `gi` + `u` (ENG-R305, ENG-R306), so every count derived from the model is one pair, six toned syllables and 92 O1 to O4 cases too high. The first version of 3.15 and ENG-T04 quoted such counts (3,368 Tier A pairs, 15,412 toned syllables, 6,209 Tier B pairs, 237,916 cases); they now quote the corrected counts (decision of the product owner, 13/11/2026, docs/decisions/0005-pa-decisions-2026-11-13.md; analysis in docs/decisions/0100-engine-syllable-counts.md).

The Swift engine's test suite is the authority; this model only exists to generate and cross-check test data.
