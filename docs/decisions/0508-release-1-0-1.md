- Status: accepted, 2026-11-22 (the integrator's record of the release; the waiver of QA-R19 is the integrator's call
  under the product owner's delegation of less urgent items, [0009](0009-pa-decisions-2026-11-18.md) item 8); the
  QA-R39 row and follow-up amended on 2026-11-23 by [0011](0011-pa-decisions-2026-11-23.md) item 3

- Runbook: [release](../runbooks/release.md), sections 2 and 5

## Context

The product owner's plan of 18/11/2026 ([0009](0009-pa-decisions-2026-11-18.md) item 1): 1.0.1 goes to the beta
channel first, and to the stable channel after at least 72 hours (QA-R29). `1.0.1-beta.1` was published on
2026-11-18 and `1.0.1-beta.2` on 2026-11-19 (about 04:20 UTC). The stable 1.0.1 is built from `43a3387e`, the commit
that moves the beta entries of `CHANGELOG.md` and `CHANGELOG.vi.md` under `## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-22`. It carries the app
code and data of `1.0.1-beta.2`: `git diff --name-only 7a4c06b6 43a3387e` lists no path under `Packages/`, `App/`,
`TestHost/`, `SpikeIMK/`, `data/`, `compat/`, `scripts/`, `spec/reference-model/`, `Config/`, `.github/` or
`project.yml`. The macOS workflows stay disabled ([0009](0009-pa-decisions-2026-11-18.md) item 6), so the gates of
QA-R30 step 1 ran on the maintainer's Mac.
