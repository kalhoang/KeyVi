# Origin: the three letter rows of the standard ANSI QWERTY layout, qwertyuiop, asdfghjkl and zxcvbnm, from the
# public layout (no other input method's data). A key's neighbours are, in this order, the two staggered keys of
# the row above (indices i and i + 1), its row mates on each side (i - 1 and i + 1) and the two staggered keys of
# the row below (i - 1 and i). The build fails unless the table is symmetric, covers the 26 letters and equals that
# derivation (lexicon-builder KeyboardNeighbours.check). Reviewed with the lists.
