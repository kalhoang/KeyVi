Notices for the word lists bundled with the Vietnamese engine data (VietnameseEngineData)
=====================================================================================

This file is generated by lexicon-builder from data/licenses/bundled-notices.txt and is shipped as
NOTICES.txt next to the word lists. Applications that bundle these resources must show the notices
below in their acknowledgements (spec/01-engine.md ENG-R811).


1. English word list (english.txt)
----------------------------------

english.txt is derived from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) release 2020.12.07 by Kevin
Atkinson, http://wordlist.aspell.net/. Only the lowercase ASCII entries of the "words" lists for
English, American and British spelling (-ise and -ize) at size levels 10, 20 and 35 are used; they
were lowercased, filtered and merged by lexicon-builder. The SCOWL copyright and permission notice
follows verbatim.

@include SCOWL-2020.12.07-Copyright.txt


2. Attested Vietnamese syllables (attested.txt)
-----------------------------------------------

attested.txt lists Vietnamese syllables that occur in Vietnamese legal normative documents
(laws and codes of the National Assembly of Viet Nam, listed in data/inputs/legal/SOURCES.tsv of the
engine's source repository), plus syllables added by the project. Legal normative documents and
their official translations are not objects of copyright protection under Article 15(2) of the Law
on Intellectual Property of Viet Nam (No. 50/2005/QH11, as amended): "Văn bản quy phạm pháp luật,
văn bản hành chính, văn bản khác thuộc lĩnh vực tư pháp và bản dịch chính thức của văn bản đó."
Only single syllables are bundled; no text of the documents is shipped.
