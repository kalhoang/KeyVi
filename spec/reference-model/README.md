# Reference model (test oracle, not shipped)

A small Python model of the orthography and composition rules in `../01-engine.md`, written by the author of that file from the spec text only (no other input method's code was consulted). It generated the corpus counts quoted in 01-engine.md section 3 and checked the worked examples.

- `ortho.py`: syllable inventory, tiers, tone rules.
- `compose.py`: keystroke-to-display composition (Telex, VNI, both tone styles).
- `boundary.py`: commit / restore decisions; `common_en.txt` is a hand-written English fixture list. `type_run` models the English run of ENG-R823 as amended on 14/11/2026 (an `en` token and no `vi` token among the previous three; a token kept by L11 is neutral), and `python3 -I boundary.py` checks rows B065-B072 of 13.9.2 and the sentences of ENG-R823 (none expected to differ). `type_run_collision` adds the collision rules at commit of 15/11/2026 (L9a boundary particle, L9b phrase looking back, the `run: vi` exemption of L10 and the English-lean window; ENG-R823, ENG-R825 to ENG-R827) with small fixture lists, and checks `COLLISION_RUNS`; the revisions of ENG-R828 are not modelled. `type_run` with `context="code"` models rule L9 as amended on 15/11/2026 (ENG-R850, REG-35: a code-only word is restored only outside Vietnamese text, read from the line before the token) with the product owner's sentence condition of 16/11/2026 (the first word of its line, after nothing but spaces and the openers of prose, in sentence case, committed by a space and not on the capitalized code-word list, is decided by the rules after L9 at its own commit, with the English run of the line before ended), and RV5 of ENG-R828 as added on 16/11/2026 (REG-36: a code-only word that L9 restored as the first word of its line takes its Vietnamese display when the next word is Vietnamese, the only revision the model covers), and checks `CODE_RUNS`, which hold rows B035 to B035e, B035i to B035n and B064a to B064c of 13.9 and 13.9.1, the sentences of ENG-R850, the sentence condition and the RV5 guards, `CODE_RUNS_WITHOUT_LINE_REVISION` (`lineStartRevision` off) and `CODE_RUNS_CAPITALIZED` (`tar` on the capitalized code-word list, standing for the bundled `host`). The 13.9 fixture lists are split as in the spec: `TECH` is the tech-always list, `CODE_ONLY` the code-only list, which holds `chown`, a word whose keys are not English, since the product owner decided on 18/11/2026 that for such a word L9 is evaluated right after L5, before L6 and L7 (ENG-R801, ENG-R850, REG-38; `CODE_RUNS` rows B035o to B035t). `decide` models rule L4 as amended by the product owner on 17/11/2026 (ENG-R804, REG-37: doublings, the keys of the escaped form typed alone, vowel groups and an escape as the last key; `compose.py` records `escape_across` and `last_escape`) and the escape condition of L3 (ENG-R801: `www` commits `www`), and checks `ESCAPE_RUNS`; the phrase evidence of ENG-R804 (a) is not modelled, and with the 13.9 lists no row has both forms English. Since 20/11/2026 `decide` also models the `w` doubling of ENG-R804 as amended by the product owner (REG-40: `compose.py` records `w_doublings`, each with whether it removed the `ư` of a standalone `w`; `R′` English is restored by (c′), and the restore of (d) puts back `R′` when the `w` doublings are a habit: every one a standalone one, no `w` key after it and none a capital right after a lowercase `w`), with `answer` and `powwow` added to its fixture lexicon for the `ESCAPE_RUNS` rows `wwebview` to `uww`. `decide` also models rule L4a as decided by the product owner on 18/11/2026 (ENG-R411, REG-39: a token whose last key removed the tone or the marks keeps the text shown unless its keys are English; `compose.py` records `last_removal`) and checks `REMOVAL_RUNS` and `REMOVAL_VNI`, rows B052a to B052d included.
- `correction.py`: the typo correction of section 11A (ENG-R1110 to ENG-R1114 and ENG-R1125, 19/11/2026): the edit model with its four position rules, the one-word rule over the correction lexicon (the bundled `english.txt` plus the letter-only tech-always entries), the lists of ENG-R1110 (c) (the guard words read exactly from the 26 pinned SCOWL lists, not from the bundled filter), the unmarked shapes and the usual-order Telex keyings generated here from the tier tables of `ortho.py` (with the three spec additions it predates: `giếc`, `Thy` and the onset `dz`), the case and context rules of ENG-R1111 and ENG-R1112, and the decision of 8.1 from `boundary.decide` pointed at the bundled lists; the typed word is the text a restore writes, `R`, or `R′` when rule L4 left out a `w` doubling (11A as amended on 20/11/2026, REG-40; with the bundled lists the model gives the engine's text for rows X28 to X39 of 13.9.5 and `wwindwos` -> `windows`). `python3 -I correction.py --self-check` compares its shapes and keyings with the bundled `unmarked-shapes.txt` and `vietnamese-keyings.txt` (equal on 19/11/2026: 3,953 and 27,277) and its table with `keyboard-neighbours.txt`, then replays the rows of 13.12 from `Tests/VietnameseEngineTests/Corpus/typo-corrections.tsv` whose 1.0 result it reproduces (77 of 84 on 19/11/2026, none differing; the rows with a restore toggle or a Backspace, the macro row and S72 are not modelled). The undo of ENG-R1117 and the revisions of ENG-R828 are not modelled.
- `correction_measure.py`: the targets of ENG-R1124 on the model, `python3 -I correction_measure.py --seed 18112026` (a few minutes): ENG-T52 injected typos (6,000 lexicon words per kind, on the rules alone as the design note measured them and with the decision of 8.1 first), ENG-T53 rare words of `/usr/share/dict/words`, ENG-T57 one-key typos of the Vietnamese words of the prose corpus typed through `compose.py` and `boundary.decide`. The design note of 18/11/2026 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-spelling-correction-design.md` section 4) measured with a script that is not in the repository; on 19/11/2026 these scripts give 85.2, 57.5, 74.9, 95.3 and 86.8 % corrected to the source (transposition, omission, insertion, doubling, neighbouring key; 18/11: 85.7, 57.5, 75.8, 95.2, 86.7) and 0, 1.35, 1.28, 0 and 0.03 % to another word (18/11: 0, 1.32, 1.20, 0, 0.05); rare words 3.16 % without the guard list (18/11: 3.42 %) and 2.74 % on the denominator of ENG-T53; 0 of 3,332 Telex typos and 0 of 570 plain-letter typos of Vietnamese words corrected, 123 and 50 without ENG-R1125 (18/11: 1 of 3,371 and 0 of 579; 133 and 53 without). The differences and their causes are in `docs/decisions/0203-correction-data.md`. The gates are ENG-T52, ENG-T53 and ENG-T57 on the engine.
- `corpus.py`: generates the O1-O4 corpus; `examples.py` checks its example list (and writes `examples.json`) and every 13.8 row of `rows.json` (`python3 -I examples.py` prints the mismatches; none expected).
- `compose.py` models Backspace by ENG-R710 as amended on 14/11/2026 (a modifier key shared with a letter that stays keeps marking it, REG-31) and the space boundary with re-open (ENG-R1002) for rows W243h and W243i; the restore decisions of section 8 are left to `boundary.py`. It also models the amendments of 15/11/2026: tone removal by layers (ENG-R411: the tone, then every mark and `đ`, REG-34), Backspace on an escape letter with the keys it undid and its fallbacks (ENG-R711, rows W241, W242, W242a-W242w: tone keys that wait for their vowel in the replay, a standalone `w` letter shown plain kept as a plain key, the letters shown retyped), the shared-key rule counting only mark keys after a mark removal (ENG-R710, W242s, W242t), and a removal key that would replay as a letter being left out (ENG-R710). The bracket doubling escape (4.8) is not modelled. Rows W243k-W243o (a re-opened restored word, ENG-R1005) are not in `rows.json`: they depend on the restore decisions of section 8.

Known gaps (update the model from the spec before relying on it):

1. It predates two review additions: rhyme `êc` after `gi` (`giếc`, ENG-R305) and open `y` after `th` (`Thy`, ENG-R314).
2. It predates the product owner's P0 additions: the `dz` onset (01-engine.md 3.3, ENG-R316), worked examples W249-W253 and boundary rows B053-B064.
3. It counts the spelling `giu` twice: once as onset `g` + rhyme `iu` and once as onset `gi` + rhyme `u`. The normative parse of 01-engine.md 3.13 reads `giu` only as `gi` + `u` (ENG-R305, ENG-R306), so every count derived from the model is one pair, six toned syllables and 92 O1 to O4 cases too high. The first version of 3.15 and ENG-T04 quoted such counts (3,368 Tier A pairs, 15,412 toned syllables, 6,209 Tier B pairs, 237,916 cases); they now quote the corrected counts (decision of the product owner, 13/11/2026, docs/decisions/0005-pa-decisions-2026-11-13.md; analysis in docs/decisions/0100-engine-syllable-counts.md).

The Swift engine's test suite is the authority; this model only exists to generate and cross-check test data.
